CaveDroid is a 2D sandbox game with pixel graphics. Mine resources, craft equipment and build in procedurally generated worlds. Survival mode includes health, hunger and combat. Creative mode provides unlimited blocks and flight for building.

Explore infinite worlds. Find plains, deserts, snowy terrain, caves, underground mineshafts and villages with residents, farms and loot. Craft boats to travel across water.

Features:
- Crafting and building: mine ores, smelt materials, craft tools and building blocks, furnish houses and store supplies in chests.
- Farming: grow crops and cook food.
- Combat: fight zombies, skeletons, spiders and creepers with swords, bows and armor. Use TNT with chain explosions.
- World simulation: flowing water and lava, and fire that spreads between blocks.
- Lighting and weather: day and night, torch lighting, rain, snowfall and freezing water in snowy areas.
- Asset packs: import custom textures, sounds and game data, including higher-resolution textures.
- Saves: keep multiple worlds and export or import saves.
- Statistics: track play time, blocks mined, items crafted and other gameplay activity.

CaveDroid is a single-player game with touch controls and can be played offline. The source code and issue tracker are available at:
https://github.com/fredboy/cavedroid
